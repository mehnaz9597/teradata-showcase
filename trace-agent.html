TRACE

An agent that finds why a model got worse, and shows its work.

Maya, a data scientist at a mid-size bank, owns the card fraud model. On Monday morning its precision has dropped from 91% to 83% and fraud ops are complaining about false positives. Today this takes her half a day across four tools. Follow her with Trace from the alert to a decision she can defend.

Main flow

Alert, plan, investigation, suspects (where Maya pushes back), root cause, decision, outcome and the record. About five minutes.

How to read the screens

  • Interface textLabels, buttons and Maya's words.
  • Trace's reasoningWhat the agent thinks. Always a judgement, never raw data.
  • Source dataValues read straight from a connected system.
  • BlueThe agent working, and its output.
  • InkMaya's decisions. Only she can approve a change.
