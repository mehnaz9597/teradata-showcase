The flow

How the prototype moves from the alert to the decision record.

  1. 01

    Cases

    The alert arrives. Precision fell from 91% to 83% and fraud ops are complaining.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Trace shows its plan and the four sources it will read. Maya starts it.

  3. 03

    Suspects

    Three possible causes with their evidence. Maya pushes back on the ranking.

  4. 04

    Root cause

    The diagnosis, how sure Trace is, what it ruled out and how it confirmed it.

  5. 05

    Decide

    The recommended fix, its risk and undo. Maya approves, rejects or asks for more.

  6. 06

    Watching

    The fix is applied by the data team. Trace checks precision recovers.

  7. 07

    Decision record

    Every step, who took it and when, for anyone who asks why.

When things aren't smooth

Branches off the main flow.

  • From step 02

    Low confidence

    A source doesn’t respond. Trace says it can’t be sure and asks to retry.

  • From step 03

    Conflicting evidence

    Two sources disagree. Trace finds out why before trusting either.

  • From step 04 or 06

    Wrong diagnosis

    Maya challenges the diagnosis. Trace re-investigates and keeps what it found.
